# The World Today — Sunday, 22 November 2026

An entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261122)
([bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261122)): sum up the
world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026 in one 1024×1024 image.

`main.py` (pure Python 3.13 standard library, no network) draws a newspaper
front page at runtime — masthead, headline, six stories and an
also-today strip — and writes it to `entry.png`:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py   # writes out/entry.png
```

Two runs produce byte-identical output. The PNG is 1024×1024 and under 1 MiB.

## The day's stories (all as reported on 22 November 2026)

- **Headline — Iran strikes Bahrain.** Tehran says its missiles and drones
  hit two US Patriot radars at al-Riffa and Awali; Bahrain says a civilian
  plant was hit and caught fire, with no injuries. Hours later a Houthi

  appealed to the UN Security Council. Sources: Reuters, Al Jazeera.

  government at a Sunday ceremony; Eisenkot invites Netanyahu's Likud to

- **Hong Kong.** 856 candidates chase 723 seats as 5,709 voters choose the

- **Myanmar.** A magnitude 4.1 earthquake strikes in the early hours of
  Sunday, India's National Centre for Seismology says. Source: ANI.
- **France.** Nationwide cost-of-living protests enter a fourth straight
  weekend. Source: The Paris Eye.
- **Sport.** Alcaraz faces Zverev in the ATP Finals title match in Turin;
  France beat Argentina to reach the rugby final. Sources: ATP Tour, BBC.
- **Also today.** US details USD 12bn in Venezuelan oil revenue; Kenya
  discharges its last Ebola patient; Al-Shabaab claims hundreds killed in
  Somalia.
